# Tutorial: understanding Section 16, “Excitatory TMEM147 connects ER/cholesterol machinery to mitochondrial signatures”

**Source section:** [Joint Phase 11–12 synthesis](phase11_phase12_joint_mitochondrial_discussion.md)  
**Audience:** readers with a high-school-level biology background  
**Purpose:** explain the proposed ER-to-mitochondria bridge and its ambiguity with SELENOW

## 1. The central idea

The endoplasmic reticulum (ER) makes and inserts many membrane proteins and
helps regulate lipids, including cholesterol. Mitochondria depend on ER lipid
and protein homeostasis. `TMEM147` participates in ER membrane-protein
machinery and has been linked to cholesterol regulation. Phase 12 repeatedly
places mitochondrial antioxidant and respiratory genes under TMEM147 in
excitatory-neuron networks. This suggests a new ER/sterol-to-mitochondria
hypothesis in AD, but no direct AD neuronal experiment confirms it.

## 2. Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ER** | Endoplasmic reticulum, a membrane network that makes proteins and lipids and helps manage calcium and stress. |
| **Translocon** | Protein machinery that inserts a newly made protein into or across a membrane. |
| **Multipass protein** | A membrane protein that crosses the membrane many times. |
| **Sterol** | A class of lipids that includes cholesterol. |
| **PRDX5** | An antioxidant enzyme found in several cellular compartments, including mitochondria. |
| **NDUFB10/NDUFB11** | Nuclear-encoded Complex I subunits. |
| **DBI** | A lipid-binding and signaling protein with roles in acyl-CoA metabolism. |
| **GADD45GIP1** | A mitochondrial ribosome-associated protein involved in mitochondrial translation. |

## 3. Discovery sentence

> **“`TMEM147` is a recurrent excitatory-neuron candidate whose neighborhoods
> contain `PRDX5`, `NDUFB10`, `NDUFB11`, `DBI`, and `GADD45GIP1`, linking
> ER/sterol machinery to the same respiratory programs identified in Phase
> 11.”**

The named genes provide a biological bridge:

- ER/cholesterol side: TMEM147 and DBI;
- redox side: PRDX5;
- Complex I side: NDUFB10 and NDUFB11;
- mitochondrial protein-production side: GADD45GIP1.

“Linking” means connected through the inferred network. Direct regulation,
physical binding, and edge direction remain untested.

## 4. Conclusion sentences

> **“This is a new AD mitochondrial-network hypothesis.”**

The targeted review did not find the exact TMEM147-centered AD neuronal
mitochondrial relationship.

> **“Known ER translocon and cholesterol functions provide a plausible bridge,
> but the targeted review found no primary AD neuronal study showing that
> TMEM147 controls OXPHOS.”**

Prior work supports TMEM147's starting functions. The final arrow to neuronal
respiration in AD is an inference, not prior confirmation.

## 5. Evidence, item by item

TMEM147 has:

- 78 total calls;
- 30 primary calls;
- five global calls; and
- 15 conservative directional calls across ten excitatory fine types.

The low global fraction—`5 / 78`, about 6.4%—means TMEM147 is rarely the most
upstream nonredundant candidate. All calls also use one broad excitatory
network, which limits their independence.

`NDUFB11` is the cleanest cross-phase readout. In Phase 11, `Exc L3-4 RORB
CUX2` shows NDUFB11 AD-up in female ε2 and AD-down in male ε2. In Phase 12,
the same gene is found under both TMEM147 and SELENOW in that context.

The exact match is useful, but two candidate parents make attribution
ambiguous: TMEM147 and SELENOW might act in the same pathway, in separate
pathways converging on NDUFB11, or simply mark the same broad network motif.

## 6. Prior work and naming warning

TMEM147 interacts with lamin B receptor and sterol-reductase machinery and
affects cholesterol homeostasis
([Christodoulou et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1242/jcs.245357)).
It is also part of a ribosome-associated ER multipass translocon
([McGilvray et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.56889)).
These functions make ER-to-mitochondria signaling plausible, but neither paper
validates AD neuronal OXPHOS control.

Nicalin is a member of a TMEM147-containing complex. **Nicalin is not
nicastrin**, the γ-secretase component. Similar names do not establish a
γ-secretase or amyloid-processing mechanism, and the current result supplies
no such evidence.

## 7. Decisive experiment

In the matched excitatory subtype, perturb:

1. TMEM147 alone;
2. SELENOW alone;
3. both together; and
4. each with gene-specific rescue.

Measure sterol composition, ER translocation, PRDX5, NDUFB10, NDUFB11,
GADD45GIP1, respiratory-complex assembly, oxygen consumption, ROS, and
viability.

If TMEM147 specifically changes ER/sterol biology first and NDUFB11/respiration
second, the bridge gains support. If only SELENOW affects the mitochondrial
readout, upstream attribution should shift. Non-additive double perturbation
could suggest a shared path; additive effects could suggest convergence.

## 8. One-sentence takeaway

TMEM147 is a plausible new ER/cholesterol-to-mitochondria candidate, but reuse
of one broad network and shared NDUFB11 placement under SELENOW make its
upstream role uncertain.
