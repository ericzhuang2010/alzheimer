# Tutorial: understanding Section 8, “GABARAPL2 connects autophagic flux to CHCHD2 and PARK7”

**Source section:** [Joint Phase 11–12 synthesis](phase11_phase12_joint_mitochondrial_discussion.md)  
**Audience:** readers with a high-school-level biology background  
**Purpose:** explain the proposed neuronal quality-control module and distinguish its confirmed and new parts

## 1. The central idea

Neurons need to recycle damaged material and remove unhealthy mitochondria.
`GABARAPL2` helps autophagosomes fuse with lysosomes, completing a major
recycling step. `CHCHD2` and `PARK7` are linked to mitochondrial maintenance
and stress protection. Phase 11 finds sex/APOE-dependent mitophagy directions,
and Phase 12 repeatedly groups these three genes in neuronal networks. Prior
experiments support parts of the module, but the complete
GABARAPL2–CHCHD2–PARK7 chain in human AD neurons is new.

## 2. Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Autophagy** | A recycling process in which cellular material is enclosed, delivered to lysosomes, and broken down. |
| **Autophagosome** | The membrane container that carries material for recycling. |
| **Autophagic flux** | The rate at which material moves through the complete autophagy pathway, not simply the number of autophagosomes. |
| **Mitophagy** | Selective autophagic removal of damaged mitochondria. |
| **Fusion** | Joining of the autophagosome with the lysosome so the cargo can be degraded. |
| **GABARAPL2** | A member of the ATG8 protein family that supports autophagosome maturation and fusion. |
| **CHCHD2** | A mitochondrial protein involved in stress responses, respiratory function, and protein quality control. |
| **PARK7/DJ-1** | A stress-protective protein associated with mitochondrial quality control and Parkinson's disease biology. |
| **Ser72** | Serine amino acid number 72 in GABARAPL2; phosphorylation at this site can alter function. |
| **Paralog** | A related gene produced by gene duplication. Related GABARAP proteins can partly compensate for one another. |

## 3. The title and novelty label

“Connects” describes co-occurrence in candidate-centered networks, not a
proven direct physical chain. “Autophagic flux” is more precise than simply
“autophagy,” because the prior AD-model result concerns restoration of a
completed recycling process.

The label separates:

- **preclinically confirmed AD biology:** changing GABARAPL2 regulation
  improves autophagic and AD-like outcomes in a mouse model;
- **new extension:** placement in specific human sex/APOE neuronal signatures,
  the GABARAPL2–PARK7 relation, and the complete three-gene module.

## 4. Discovery sentence

> **“Phase 11's subgroup-sensitive mitophagy phenotype gains a Phase 12
> neuronal quality-control neighborhood centered on `GABARAPL2` and
> recurrently containing `CHCHD2` and `PARK7`.”**

Phase 11 provides the phenotype-level observation: mitophagy-related RNA
changes differ by group. Phase 12 provides a candidate network that may help
organize that phenotype. “Gains” means the combined interpretation adds a
possible network structure; it does not mean Phase 12 experimentally caused
or rescued mitophagy.

## 5. Conclusion paragraph

> **“GABARAPL2-dependent fusion is experimentally validated in an AD model,
> and a separate non-AD molecular study supports the GABARAP–CHCHD2
> relationship.”**

Two edges have precedent:

1. GABARAPL2 regulation can change autophagic flux and AD-like outcomes in an
   animal model.
2. GABARAP-family proteins can associate with CHCHD2/CHCHD10 in a molecular
   system not designed as a human AD validation study.

> **“What remains new is the human sex/APOE-stratified neuronal placement, the
> `GABARAPL2`–`PARK7` relationship, and the complete three-gene module.”**

No cited experiment establishes that the module operates together in the
specific human neuronal groups reported here. PARK7 may be downstream,
parallel, or merely co-located in the network.

## 6. Evidence, item by item

### Phase 11 mitophagy directions

- Female ε2: `23 up / 1 down`, overwhelmingly AD-up.
- Male ε2: `14 up / 46 down`, predominantly AD-down.
- Female ε4: additional loss of quality-control-related expression.

These are gene–cell-type occurrences, not numbers of people or unique genes.
The female-versus-male ε2 contrast makes the phenotype
“subgroup-sensitive.”

### Phase 12 GABARAPL2 recurrence

- 79 total calls
- 32 primary calls
- excitatory and inhibitory neuronal networks
- 13 fine cell types
- 17 conservative directional calls across 11 fine types
- every primary call is candidate-self-independent

Self-independence strengthens candidate nomination because GABARAPL2 was not
already in the query responsible for each primary call.

### The proposed module

- `CHCHD2` occurs in 25 primary GABARAPL2 neighborhoods across ten fine types.
- `PARK7` occurs in 16 primary neighborhoods across seven fine types.

The neighborhoods span female-ε2 AD-up, female-ε4 AD-down, both directions in
male ε2, male-ε3/ε3 AD-down, and male-ε4 AD-up. This breadth argues that the
module is recurrent, but it also shows that the network does not have one
universal AD direction.

## 7. Prior work

GABARAP-family proteins are required for autophagosome–lysosome fusion
([Wang et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1507263112)).
In 5xFAD mice, SIK2-dependent phosphorylation of GABARAPL2 at Ser72 restored
autophagic flux and improved amyloid, synaptic, and behavioral phenotypes
([Dai et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40035-025-00514-4)).

CHCHD2 and CHCHD10 preferentially associate with GABARAP-family proteins and
can promote aggregate clearance
([Zhou et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1080/15548627.2026.2678427)).
PARK7 participates in PINK1/Parkin-related mitophagy
([Imberechts et al., 2022](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awac313)), but the
review did not locate a direct GABARAPL2–PARK7 study.

More broadly, increasing mitophagy improves amyloid, tau, and cognition in AD
models ([Fang et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-018-0332-9)), and
restoring neuronal PINK1 is beneficial in AD models
([Du et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awx258)).

These findings support quality-control biology, not the exact human module.

## 8. Limits and decisive experiment

GABARAP-family paralogs overlap in function, so removing only GABARAPL2 may be
partly masked by compensation. RNA-level KDA also cannot detect whether Ser72
is phosphorylated.

A useful experiment would:

1. alter GABARAPL2 abundance and separately alter its Ser72 phosphorylation
   state in APOE- and sex-contextualized excitatory neurons;
2. measure completed autophagosome–lysosome fusion and mitophagic flux;
3. measure CHCHD2 and PARK7 RNA, protein, location, and interaction;
4. measure respiration, amyloid/tau-related outcomes, synaptic function, and
   viability; and
5. remove PARK7 to test whether it is required for the GABARAPL2 response.

Rescue is essential: restoring normal GABARAPL2 or PARK7 function should
reverse the phenotype if the proposed chain is correct.

## 9. One-sentence takeaway

Experimental work supports GABARAPL2-mediated autophagic control and a
GABARAP–CHCHD2 relationship, while the PARK7 connection and the complete
sex/APOE-specific human neuronal module remain new hypotheses.
