The Mount Sinai Brain Bank (MSBB) cohort is a distinct collection of postmortem human brain donors and associated molecular/clinical data.
In this paper:
Primary/discovery analysis: ROSMAP single-nucleus RNA-seq from the prefrontal cortex.
Independent validation: Previously generated MSBB bulk RNA-seq from the parahippocampal gyrus.
Validation goal: Test whether genes showing sex-dependent AD effects in the ROSMAP single-cell analysis were enriched among sex-specific AD genes in MSBB.
So it is a separate cohort, but the validation is not a direct single-cell replication: it uses different donors, a different brain region, and bulk rather than single-nucleus RNA-seq. It validates mainly the sex-specific transcriptional signal, not every cell-type- or APOE-specific result. The reported enrichment was also modest and depended on adjusting DEG thresholds for unequal statistical power between women and men.
