# Phase 19b regional GWAS screen

This validated WS2 bundle is a complete current-source scan of Bellenguez 2022
clinical AD and three CSF biomarker GWAS files across all 226 mapped Phase 19b
gene windows. X-chromosome rows retain the source-specific structural status.

Regional hits are proximity annotations and coloc gates only. They are not
gene-level genetic support by themselves.
