# Methods: RPL11 astrocyte consensus network

The three conservative-support RPL11 rows were read from `call_key_driver_returns.tsv`. For each run, the astrocyte Bayesian network was restricted to the recorded effective background, and the downstream RPL11 neighborhood was reconstructed through that run's selected final layer. A node's neighborhood-occurrence count is the number of these three run-specific neighborhoods containing it. Mitochondrial query-hit recurrence was counted from the published overlap genes; the display threshold was lowered from 2/3 to 1/3 so every observed query hit is retained.

The expanded network contains 18 nodes and 17 edges. It includes all direct RPL11 edges—one incoming (`RPLP1 → RPL11`) and three outgoing (`RPL11 → COX7C`, `RPL11 → CWC15`, and `RPL11 → PRDX1`)—plus the second upstream step `RPS25 → RPLP1`. The 12 displayed mitochondrial query hits are `ATP5F1E`, `ATP5ME`, `ATP5PF`, `COX6C`, `COX7C`, `CYB5R3`, `NDUFB4`, `PSAP`, `SLIRP`, `TOMM7`, `UQCRB`, and `UQCRH`. `DYNLL1` and other non-hit nodes are retained when required as direct neighbors or shortest-path connectors.

Pathway over-representation used MSigDB C2:CP v2026.1 human gene symbols. The custom background comprised 5,769 astrocyte Bayesian-network genes represented in the library; all 18 displayed genes mapped. One-sided hypergeometric tests were run for 1,594 pathways containing 15–500 background genes, followed by Benjamini–Hochberg correction. ETC/OXPHOS and cristae formation meet BH FDR < 0.05 with at least three displayed members. Cytosolic ribosome is retained as a prespecified contextual annotation because it identifies RPL11 and its upstream ribosomal chain; its expanded-set BH FDR is 0.0805.

The graph was rendered in Cytoscape 3.10.4. RPL11 was fixed at the center; upstream levels U1 and U2 were placed 230 and 420 Cytoscape units to the left, while downstream levels D1, D2, and D3 used radii of 260, 470, and 680 units. Pairwise node-clearance checks were applied before export. Guide rings were not drawn. PNG was exported at 300% zoom, with PDF and SVG retained as vector formats and the `.cys` session saved for editing.
