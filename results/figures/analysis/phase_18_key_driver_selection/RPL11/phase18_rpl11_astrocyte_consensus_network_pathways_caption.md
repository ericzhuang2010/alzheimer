# RPL11 astrocyte consensus network with pathway outlines

RPL11-centered astrocyte Bayesian-network neighborhood reconstructed across the three Phase 18 runs that provided conservative RPL11 support. Arrows follow the recorded Bayesian-network direction and should be interpreted as model-derived regulatory hypotheses, not experimental proof of direct regulation. The display retains the complete upstream chain `RPS25 → RPLP1 → RPL11`, all three direct RPL11 targets, every mitochondrial query hit present in at least one of the three supporting runs, and the shortest paths connecting RPL11 to those hits.

The 1/3 display threshold produces 18 nodes and 17 edges, including 12 mitochondrial query hits. For downstream nodes, node size and the printed `x/3` value show how many supporting runs contained the gene in the run-specific selected RPL11 neighborhood. This is distinct from query-hit recurrence. The upstream nodes are labeled `U1` and `U2`; they provide context and were not counted in downstream KDA neighborhoods. Thick black borders identify mitochondrial query hits meeting the 1/3 display threshold. Node fill summarizes stored Phase 8 direct-DEG direction: light yellow, both AD-up and AD-down; light blue, AD-down only; orange, AD-up only; gray, no stored direct DEG.

Colored outer boundaries mark three nonredundant pathway representatives with at least three displayed members. Blue denotes ETC/oxidative phosphorylation (8 genes; BH FDR = 1.98 × 10⁻⁸), and green denotes cristae formation (`ATP5F1E`, `ATP5ME`, and `ATP5PF`; BH FDR = 0.00475). Dark violet marks the prespecified cytosolic-ribosome context (`RPL11`, `RPLP1`, and `RPS25`); after expanding the displayed gene set its BH FDR is 0.0805, so it is contextual rather than significant at 0.05. Absence of an outline means only that a gene is not in these three displayed representatives.
